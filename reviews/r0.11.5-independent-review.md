## Findings

### Critical (Must Fix)

### Important (Should Fix)

### 1. VALID — Minor — `/docs/` and `/redoc/` prefixes treat `..` as public

- File: `backend/app/api/auth.py` (`is_public_path`, `_PUBLIC_PREFIXES`)
- What's wrong: `is_public_path("/docs/../portfolio")` and `is_public_path("/redoc/../api/v1/risk/runs")` are True. Encoded lookalikes (`/docs/%2e%2e/portfolio`) are also True.
- Why it matters: This is a prefix smell, not a working data bypass today. TestClient normalizes `..` to `/portfolio` and the gate returns 401. Raw ASGI with an unnormalized `/docs/../portfolio` is allowed through the middleware and **404s** — Starlette 1.6 does not collapse `..` onto `/portfolio`. `/api/v1/health/../portfolio` is not public and is 401.
- Suggested fix: Reject any public path whose decoded segments contain `..`, or allow-list exact docs/redoc asset paths instead of a prefix.

### 2. VALID — Minor — Shared-profile tests lock `/portfolio`, not `/risk` or `/runs`

- File: `backend/tests/test_shared_auth.py`
- What's wrong: Authenticated/unauthenticated assertions are `/health`, `/portfolio`, `/api/v1/portfolio`, docs, OpenAPI, and Compose text. Attack 4’s routes are ungated in the suite.
- Why it matters: A later change that skipped the middleware for the risk-runs router would not fail `test_shared_auth.py`. I probed those routes; they are 401 now.
- Suggested fix: One unauthenticated 401 and one Bearer 2xx/4xx-not-401 on `GET /api/v1/risk/runs/{id}` and `POST /api/v1/risk/summary` (legacy + `/api/v1`).
