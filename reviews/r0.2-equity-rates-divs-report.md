R0.2 equity-family settlement rate and dividend-yield authority

Quant Pricing Engineer

## Summary
When a `MarketSnapshot` is supplied, `EquityFuturePosition` and
`EuropeanOptionPosition` now require a settlement-currency rate in
`market.rates` and a symbol dividend yield in `market.dividend_yields` in both
`BuiltinPricingEngine` and the QuantLib overlay. A missing rate raises
`MissingMarketDataError` with `factor_key="rates[<ccy>]"`; a missing yield
raises with `factor_key="dividend_yields[<symbol>]"`. Equity instruments have
no contractual currency field, so the lookup key is `"USD"` (via
`getattr(position, "currency", None)` with that default). Trade-local
`risk_free_rate` and `dividend_yield` remain only when `market is None`. Spot
and option-vol authority from prior slices are unchanged. FX, bond, swap, and
IR fallbacks were not removed.

## Public/interface changes
- No DTO, API, or snapshot schema change.
- Production pricing contract for equity futures and European options: a
  supplied snapshot must contain `rates[<settlement ccy>]` (USD today) and
  `dividend_yields[symbol]`, or valuation raises `MissingMarketDataError`.
- `MarketSnapshot.rates` still defaults to `{"USD": 0.04}`. A snapshot that
  omits the `rates=` argument is therefore not a missing-USD-rate case; tests
  that isolate a missing rate pass `rates={}`.

## Numerical conventions
- Units: future PV remains `quantity * multiplier * F`; option PV remains
  Black–Scholes unit price times `quantity`. Carry still uses continuous
  `exp((r-q)*T)`.
- Sign convention: unchanged (higher r raises long-future / long-call PV;
  higher q lowers both).
- Day count/calendar if relevant: unchanged; this slice is rate/div
  authority only.
- Tolerances/reference: authority tests assert inequality versus trade-local
  marks, unchanged `model_dump`, and typed `factor_key`. No new golden PV.

## Known limitations / risks
- Other instrument families (FX, bond, swap, IR) still use `.get` /
  trade-mark fallbacks, including FX option vol and FX rates.
- `market is None` still prices from embedded marks (compatibility path).
- Equity instruments still assume USD unless a `currency` attribute is
  added later; `domain/models.py` was not changed.
- `MarketSnapshot.rates` still defaults to `{"USD": 0.04}`. Omitting
  `rates=` is not the same as a missing USD rate.
- Suites outside the required list that already price equity futures/options
  against a supplied snapshot (for example `test_attribution.py`,
  `test_full_reval_golden.py`) will now raise unless those fixtures seed
  `dividend_yields[symbol]`. They were not in this slice's required commands.
