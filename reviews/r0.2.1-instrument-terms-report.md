## Summary
Introduced frozen, type-discriminated `InstrumentTerms` models for every
production position family already handled by `trade_cache_key`.
`terms_from_position(position)` copies contractual economics only
(identifier, instrument type, quantity/notional, currency or pair, strike,
maturity, pay/receive, multiplier, contractual fixed rate, `pv01`, payment
frequency, option side, and hierarchy placement). Live observables already
excluded by the cache projection (`price`, `spot`, `vol`, `yield_rate`,
`market_swap_rate`, `quoted_rate`, `forward_rate`, `discount_rate`,
`domestic_rate`, `foreign_rate`, `risk_free_rate`, `dividend_yield`,
`forward_swap_rate`, derived `duration`) are not fields on terms. Unknown
families raise `TypeError`. Compatibility `*Position` DTOs were not edited
and still carry marks. Cache identity and pricing `value()` were not rewired.
RF-001 remains open.

## Public/interface changes
- New module `app.domain.instrument_terms` with family models:
  `EquityTerms`, `EquityFutureTerms`, `EuropeanOptionTerms`, `BondTerms`,

  (`TypeAdapter` with `type` discriminator) for wire validation.
- `terms_from_position(position) -> InstrumentTerms`.
- Equity cash/future/option terms add contractual settlement `currency`

  field). No DTO, API, cache, or pricing interface change.
- Position DTOs unchanged: marks remain on compatibility models.

## Numerical conventions
- Units: none computed. Terms are structural contractual fields only.
- Sign convention: signed quantity / notional / `pay_fixed` copied as stored
  on the Position; no P&L or risk sign is applied.
- Day count/calendar if relevant: not applicable (maturities remain year
  fractions already on the Position).
- Tolerances/reference: exact structural equality of Pydantic models. Two
  Positions that differ only in excluded marks must produce identical terms;
  a change to strike, maturity, notional, or quantity must produce different
  terms. No invented pricing or risk numbers.
