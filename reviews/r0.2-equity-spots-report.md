# Agent Handoff Template

## Task
R0.2 equity-family spots continuation (futures and European options)

## Owner
Quant Pricing Engineer

## Summary
When a `MarketSnapshot` is supplied, `EquityFuturePosition` and `EuropeanOptionPosition`
now require `market.equity_spots[symbol]` in both `BuiltinPricingEngine` and the
QuantLib overlay. A missing key raises `MissingMarketDataError` with
`factor_key="equity_spots[<symbol>]"`, matching cash equity. Trade-local spots remain
only when `market is None`. Vol, rates, and dividend-yield `.get` fallbacks are
unchanged. FX, bond, swap, and IR fallbacks were not removed.

## Public/interface changes
- No DTO, API, or snapshot schema change.
- Production pricing contract for equity futures and European options: a supplied
  snapshot must contain `equity_spots[symbol]` or valuation raises
  `MissingMarketDataError`.

## Numerical conventions
- Units: future PV remains `quantity * multiplier * F`; option PV remains Black–Scholes
  unit price times `quantity`.
- Sign convention: unchanged (long future/call positive in rising spot).
- Day count/calendar if relevant: unchanged; this slice is spot authority only.
- Tolerances/reference: authority tests assert inequality versus trade-local marks
  and unchanged `model_dump`; no new golden PV.
