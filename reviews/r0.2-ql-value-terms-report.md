## Task
R0.2 leftover — wire `QuantLibPricingEngine.value` through `InstrumentTerms`

## Owner
Quant Pricing Engineer

## Summary
`QuantLibPricingEngine.value` now projects the incoming `Position` through
`terms_from_position` and uses those contractual fields (quantity, strike,
maturity, symbol, pair, currency, notional, and family-specific economics)
as the instrument identity. When a `MarketSnapshot` is supplied, live marks
(spots, vols, rates, quotes) come only from that snapshot. Leftover DTO
marks on the same two trades no longer change PV. Quantity and strike still
move PV. Unknown types still raise `TypeError` with the existing
`unsupported instrument for QuantLib production pricing` message.
`PricingEngine.value` still takes `Position`. Compatibility `*Position` DTO
marks were not stripped. Evaluation-date / process-lock behavior is
unchanged. RF-001 remains open.

## Public/interface changes
- `QuantLibPricingEngine.value(self, position: Position, market=...)` is
  unchanged at the ABC / call-site boundary.
- Internally, `value()` calls `terms_from_position(position)`, stamps
  terms economics onto a working copy, and overlays snapshot marks when
  `market` is not `None`.
- No DTO, API, cache, Builtin, or `InstrumentTerms` module change.

## Numerical conventions
- Units: unchanged. Equity PV remains `quantity * spot`; option PV remains
  Black–Scholes–Merton unit price times `quantity`; other families keep
  their existing QuantLib / algebraic conventions.
- Sign convention: unchanged (long cash/call positive in rising spot).
- Day count/calendar if relevant: unchanged. This slice does not touch
  `_maturity_date`, `Settings.evaluationDate`, or the process lock.
- Tolerances/reference: snapshot-authority cases use `rel=1e-12, abs=1e-9`
  against the snapshot-implied cash PV (`qty * equity_spots[symbol]`).
  Option ATM vs OTM is an inequality invariant (lower strike call worth
  more). The terms-wiring case doubles `EquityTerms.quantity` and requires
  exact doubled snapshot PV.

## Tests added/updated
- `test_dto_mark_divergence_does_not_change_snapshot_equity_pv` — two
  cash equities that differ only in leftover DTO `price` price identically
  from `equity_spots`.
- `test_dto_mark_divergence_does_not_change_snapshot_option_pv` — leftover
  option `spot` / `volatility` do not win over the snapshot.
- `test_terms_quantity_and_strike_change_snapshot_pv` — doubling quantity
  doubles equity PV; lower strike call is worth more than a higher strike.
- `test_value_uses_terms_quantity_not_dto_when_terms_diverge` — patched
  `terms_from_position` quantity is the economics identity (would stay at
  DTO quantity if `value()` never called terms).
- Process-state suite left as-is; all existing assertions still pass.
