## Findings

### 1. VALID — Minor — `to_stress_scenario` still collapses two USD tenors to one currency key

- Files: `backend/app/risk/scenarios.py` L274–296
- What's wrong: The new panel helpers build a `MarketScenario` with two
  `RateZero` shocks (`2Y` 0.001, `10Y` −0.0025). `to_stress_scenario` writes
  `rate_shocks_bps[factor.currency] = amount * 10000`, so the second tenor
  overwrites the first. Probe: `{"USD": -25.0}`. That is the old four-macro
  currency bag.
- Why it matters: Anyone who converts a panel scenario through this helper
  and `shock_snapshot` loses tenor independence. It is **not** on the claimed
  opt-in risk path (`iter_panel_shocked_snapshots` / `apply_market_scenario`
  / `bump`). No production caller of `to_stress_scenario` was added. Default
  historical replay is unchanged.
- Suggested fix: Leave the helper on aggregate scenarios, or extend
  `StressScenario` with per-tenor maps before any panel caller uses it.
  Optional. Not required to keep this leftover.
