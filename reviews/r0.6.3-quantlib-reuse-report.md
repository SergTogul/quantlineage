# R0.6.3 QuantLib Reuse Report

## Task

R0.6.3 - Reuse QuantLib structures where safe.

## Owner

Quant Pricing Engineer.

## Summary

Implemented adapter-local reuse in `QuantLibPricingEngine` without changing the `PricingEngine` interface or full-revaluation methodology. The full-revaluation loop still reprices every trade under every shocked snapshot, but scalar equity and FX options no longer rebuild the same QuantLib option structure for each scenario.

## What Is Reused

- Contract terms are cached per engine from the Pydantic position payload. These terms exclude live market marks and are safe to reuse across shocked snapshots.
- Scalar equity and FX option paths cache the QuantLib payoff, European exercise, `VanillaOption`, BSM process, flat curve structures, and constant-vol term structure by contract terms and evaluation date.
- Scalar option market inputs are live `SimpleQuote` objects. Every valuation updates spot, rate, dividend/foreign rate, and volatility quotes before calling `NPV()` / Greeks.
- Vanilla swap fixed/floating schedules are cached by currency, maturity, and evaluation date. The swap instrument, curve handle, index, and fixings are still rebuilt per valuation.

## What Is Rebuilt

- Attached equity/FX volatility surfaces are rebuilt from the current `MarketSnapshot` because surface grids and strike mapping depend on shocked market state.
- Snapshot curve/key-rate handles are rebuilt from the current `MarketSnapshot`; scalar option flat curves are reused only through live quote handles.
- Bonds, swaps, cap/floor, swaption, futures, and forwards keep their existing pricing math except for cached swap schedules and cached terms.
- QuantLib sessions remain serialized by the process lock. This slice does not add in-process threading, process partitioning, or native kernels.

## Numerical Conventions

- No pricing convention changed.
- Option vega remains QuantLib vega converted to one vol point via `* 0.01`.
- Cash delta/gamma scaling remains unchanged for equity and FX options.
- Evaluation-date-sensitive structures are keyed by ISO evaluation date to avoid stale maturity dates.

## Tests

Added `backend/tests/test_quantlib_reuse.py`:

- `test_scalar_option_full_reval_reuses_vanilla_option_structure` monkeypatches `ql.VanillaOption` and proves base plus four shocked full-revaluation scenarios construct one scalar option structure instead of five.
- `test_scalar_option_full_reval_reuse_matches_cold_valuations` compares reused full-revaluation P&L to a cold path that creates a fresh QuantLib engine for each shocked valuation. Tolerance: absolute `1e-12`.

Existing focused pricing and full-revaluation suites passed unchanged after implementation.

## RF-007 Status

RF-007 remains `IN PROGRESS`. This slice reduces avoidable QuantLib structure rebuilds where safe, but it does not close N x S full-revaluation pricing, contribution reuse, process partitioning, or cache-policy work.

## Known Limitations / Risks

- Reuse cache is per `QuantLibPricingEngine` instance and intentionally not exposed outside the adapter.
- Attached surface and curve structures remain rebuilt from each shocked snapshot to avoid stale market state.
- This is structural reuse proof, not an end-to-end performance SLA.
- The repo currently has unrelated mypy failures outside Quant Pricing ownership.
