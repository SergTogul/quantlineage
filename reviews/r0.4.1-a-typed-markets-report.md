## Numerical conventions
- Units:
  - Curve shifts: input basis points → absolute decimal zero via
    `bps_to_decimal_rate` (`25` bp → `0.0025`).
  - Rates / zeros remain continuously compounded absolute decimals.
- Sign convention: unchanged (positive bp raises zeros).
- Day count/calendar if relevant: Actual/365-style year fractions (existing).
- Tolerances/reference: existing curve suite; shifts pinned to helper output.
