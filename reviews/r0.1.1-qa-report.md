## Mutation coverage

The tests reject:

- the complementary 5% loss quantile in place of VaR95;
- an `int(0.95 * n)` non-interpolated percentile index;
- the 95% P&L quantile without converting to losses;
- ES from the best observation or from all observations;
- strict `loss > VaR` membership in place of `loss >= VaR`.

The membership test uses losses `0..20` at 95% confidence. Linear quantile
lands exactly on loss `19`; `>=` produces ES `19.5`, while strict `>` produces
`20.0`.
