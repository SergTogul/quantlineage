## Summary
`StressEngine.run` and `StressEngine.evaluate` now call `apply_scenario(market,
scenario)` once per scenario, then revalue every position with
`PricingEngine.value` on that shared shocked snapshot. The same once-per-scenario
pattern is applied in `ScenarioAttributionEngine` via `_portfolio_pnl_by_position`
(including factor-isolated sub-scenarios). `PricingEngine.shocked_value` semantics
are unchanged for single-position callers. RF-006 and RF-004 stay **IN PROGRESS**
(not closed).
