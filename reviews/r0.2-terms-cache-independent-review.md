projection. Bond `yield_rate` / `duration`, swap `market_swap_rate` /
`duration`, FX spot-vol-rates, and IR quotes / forwards / discount /

position leaves the fingerprint equal; changing notional, strike,

is gone from `backend/`. Position DTOs are unchanged.

the current `Position` union. It is **not** enough to close RF-001:
there are still no typed `InstrumentTerms` models, and compatibility
`*Position` DTOs still carry embedded marks. FINDINGS remains
**IN PROGRESS** with remaining work “InstrumentTerms split.”

## Findings

### 1. VALID — Minor — Exclude-list, not allow-list

- File: `backend/app/pricing/cache.py` (`trade_cache_key`)
- What's wrong: Economics are `model_dump` minus a per-family
  observable set. A new mark field added to an existing `*Position`
  would enter the key until someone extends the set. A new union
  member already fail-closes (`TypeError`).
- Why it matters: This is the same pattern as the HEAD equity
  projections, not a current leak. Typed `InstrumentTerms` (remaining
  RF-001) would replace the subtract with an explicit contractual
  payload.
- Suggested fix: Defer to the InstrumentTerms split. Do not treat this
  as open cache-identity work.

### 2. VALID — Minor — FX-option rate marks omitted from parametrize

- File: `backend/tests/test_pricing_cache.py`
  (`test_trade_key_excludes_snapshot_marks_but_keeps_economics`)
- What's wrong: FX-option `mark_update` is `{spot, volatility}` only.
  `domestic_rate` / `foreign_rate` are excluded in production (probed)
  but a restore of those two fields into the key would not fail that
  parametrize row.
- Why it matters: Coverage hole, not a leftover hash. Bond/swap have a
  second dedicated test; FX forward does include both rates.
- Suggested fix: Add the two FX-option rates to that row’s
  `mark_update`.

## Cache identity vs RF-001

R0.2.4 requires trade economics + snapshot identity + pricing
configuration, and forbids hashing duplicated trade-local marks. For
the current ten-type `Position` union, that is now true: no brief
observable remains in the fingerprint, and unknown families cannot
silently fall back to a full dump.

RF-001 acceptance still requires typed Trade / InstrumentTerms,
snapshot-only market authority, and removal of embedded marks from the
target trade contract. Those models do not exist (`InstrumentTerms` /
`*Terms` classes: no hits under `backend/`). Compatibility DTOs still
carry `yield_rate`, `market_swap_rate`, spots, vols, and IR quotes.
Builtin / QuantLib may still read those fields on `market is None`
compatibility paths. That is outside this slice and is why RF-001 must
stay open.

**Treat R0.2.4 cache identity as done. Do not close RF-001.**
