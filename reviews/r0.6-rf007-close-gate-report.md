## Why CLOSE (not KEEP OPEN)

| Exit word | Judgment |
|---|---|

RF-008 precedent: architecture in place, core acceptance met, remaining required-direction items (limits, lazy drilldown) named as P1. Here the missing bench matrix is acceptance-evidence remainder after architecture + numerical identity, not a still-open reconstruction root cause.

Task 4 review warning (do not close on replica-count if N=100/1k wall-clock is *required*): accepted. This close does **not** use replica count as evidence. N=100/1k stays P1. Option B job isolation is the bound, not a throughput claim.
