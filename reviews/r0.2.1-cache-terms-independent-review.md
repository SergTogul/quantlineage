## Findings

### 1. VALID — Minor — Equity-family `currency` pop is a leftover subtract

- File: `backend/app/pricing/cache.py` (`trade_cache_key`)
- What's wrong: After switching to the allow-list terms dump, equity /
  equity-future / european-option still `pop("currency")` so
  `equity_terms_v1` / `equity_future_terms_v1` / `equity_option_terms_v1`
  stay byte-identical to the HEAD deny-list payload. Settlement
  currency is contractual economics on `InstrumentTerms` (brief §1)
  and is already a terms field.
- Why it matters: Not a mark leak. Equity `*Position` DTOs still have
  no `currency` field, so two live trades cannot differ only in
  settlement currency today. Including the field under the same
  schema id would silently change every equity cache key. The pop is
  the v1 compatibility mechanism the slice claimed.
- Suggested fix: Defer to an explicit `equity_terms_v2` (and
  future/option equivalents) when equity DTOs gain a currency field
  or when `value()` is wired. Do not treat this as open mark-leakage
  work.

### 2. VALID — Minor — Incomplete `mark_update` on two option rows

- File: `backend/tests/test_pricing_cache.py`
  (`test_trade_key_excludes_snapshot_marks_but_keeps_economics`)
- What's wrong: European-option `mark_update` omits `dividend_yield`.
  FX-option `mark_update` is `{spot, volatility}` only. Those fields
  are excluded in production (probed) but a restore into the key
  would not fail those parametrize rows.
- Why it matters: Coverage hole, not a leftover hash. Dedicated
  identity goldens would also miss a mark that is absent from the
  fixture dump used to compute the golden.
- Suggested fix: Add `dividend_yield` to the equity-option row and
  the two FX-option rates to that row’s `mark_update`.
