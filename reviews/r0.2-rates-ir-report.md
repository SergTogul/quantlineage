## Summary
When a `MarketSnapshot` is supplied, bonds, swaps, IR futures, caps/floors,
and swaptions now require snapshot marks in both `BuiltinPricingEngine` and
the QuantLib overlay. Missing keys raise `MissingMarketDataError` with
`factor_key="rates[<ccy>]"` (bond/swap/IR discount),
`"projection_rates[<ccy>]"` (IR-future / cap / swaption forward when no
curve, projection scalar, or discount scalar is present),
`"ir_future_quotes[<ccy>]"`, or `"ir_vols[<ccy>]"` (cap/floor/swaption; a
usable `vol_surfaces[<ccy>]` quote also satisfies vol). Trade-local
`yield_rate` / `market_swap_rate` / `quoted_rate` / `forward_rate` /
`forward_swap_rate` / `discount_rate` / `volatility` remain only when
`market is None`. FX and equity fail-closed behavior was not changed.
Canned `rates-macro` demo and aggregate snapshots now seed
`ir_future_quotes["USD"]=0.042` so the STIR future still prices.

## Public/interface changes
- No API/DTO rewrite. `MarketSnapshot` gained two optional maps with empty
  defaults: `ir_vols` and `ir_future_quotes` (currency-keyed). They are
  frozen, serialized, and included in `content_hash`.
- Production pricing contract for bonds/swaps: a supplied snapshot must
  contain a currency curve / `key_rates` or `rates[<ccy>]`.
- IR futures: same resolution for the forward (`prefer_projection=True`)
  plus `ir_future_quotes[<ccy>]`.
- Caps/floors/swaptions: discount from curve/`rates`, forward from
  projection curve/`projection_rates`/`rates`, vol from
  `vol_surfaces[<ccy>]` or `ir_vols[<ccy>]`.
- `MarketSnapshot.rates` still defaults to `{"USD": 0.04}`. Tests that
  isolate a missing USD rate pass `rates={}`.
- Demo adapter seeds `ir_future_quotes` from IR-future `quoted_rate` and
  `ir_vols` from cap/floor/swaption `volatility`.

## Numerical conventions
- Units: bond PV remains face × quantity × DF; swap PV remains
  `sign * (par - fixed) * notional * duration`; STIR PV remains
  `quantity * pv01 * (quoted - forward) * 10000`; cap/floor/swaption remain
  scoped Black-76 with vega per 1 vol point.
- Sign convention: unchanged (higher yield lowers long bond; higher par
  raises payer swap; higher forward lowers long STIR; higher vol raises
  long cap/payer).
- Day count/calendar if relevant: unchanged; scalar bond path still uses
  continuous Actual365Fixed year fraction. This slice is market-authority
  only.
- Tolerances/reference: authority tests assert Builtin complete-snapshot
  vs trade-local overlay `rel=1e-12`; QuantLib vs Builtin bond/STIR
  `rel=1e-12`, cap/swaption `rel=1e-12` / `abs=1e-8`. No golden PV
  rewritten.

## Known limitations / risks
- `ir_vols` / `ir_future_quotes` are currency-keyed scalars. A vol cube
  keyed by product/expiry/tenor remains out of scope (architecture brief
  family F). Distinct USD tenors still share one quote/vol key if seeded
  through the demo adapter.
- Projection still accepts `rates[<ccy>]` as a last scalar source when no
  projection curve / `projection_rates` entry exists (same
  `continuous_zero` order as before). Isolate a missing forward with
  `rates={}` as well as empty `projection_rates`.
- `MarketSnapshot.rates` still defaults to `{"USD": 0.04}`. Omitting
  `rates=` is not a missing-USD-rate case.
- `market is None` still prices from embedded marks (compatibility path;
  omitted-`market=` inference was out of scope).
- Bond/swap `duration` remains a legacy DV01 shortcut, not a market mark.
- Cache identity for these families remains the legacy position payload
  (embedded marks still hashed). Terms fingerprint was not migrated.
- Adding the two maps changes `content_hash` JSON shape versus pre-slice
  snapshots (empty dicts are now hashed). In-repo tests compare live hashes
  and stayed green.
