## Finding 1 — Frontend non-root untested — CLOSED

Original: Acc 3 required a frontend non-root drop; pytest did not fail
if `USER nginx` was deleted, commented, or left only in the `build`
stage, or if `nginx.conf` (pid/temp, no `user`) was removed.

Now:

- `test_frontend_final_stage_drops_to_non_root_user` parses **logical**
  instructions (continuations joined; full-line comments dropped), takes
  the stage after the last `FROM`, and requires that stage’s last `USER`
  identity (before `:`) is not `root` / `0`.
- `test_frontend_nginx_conf_is_non_root_safe` requires `pid` and all
  five `*_temp_path` directives under `/tmp` or `/tmp/…`, and rejects a
  real `user` directive. `#` comments are stripped first.
- Helper unit tests lock comment-only `USER` and build-stage-only
  `USER` to an empty final-stage user list.

On disk: last `FROM` is `nginx:alpine`; last `USER` is `nginx` after
`chown`. `nginx.conf` has `pid /tmp/nginx.pid`, the five temp paths
under `/tmp`, logs under `/tmp`, `listen 80`, and no `user` directive.
