# R0.2 Architecture Brief — Separate Trade Economics from Market State (RF-001)

Status: implementation brief; slice R0.2-A implemented and independently APPROVE; remaining family slices OPEN  
Branch baseline: `r0-core-remediation`  
Scope: bounded R0.2 migration, preserving temporary compatibility and existing numerical behavior  
Primary owner: Lead Architect; handoffs to Quant Pricing, Market Data, Portfolio Risk, and QA

## Decision and boundary

The production valuation contract is:

```text
Trade / InstrumentTerms = contractual economics only
MarketSnapshot          = the sole authoritative observable market state
PricingEngine.value(trade, market) = the production risk path
```

No production risk calculation may infer a market from trades or call pricing without an explicit
snapshot. A separately named demo/test adapter may seed a snapshot from legacy sample marks, but it
must detect competing definitions rather than overwrite them. Existing `*Position` models remain
accepted at API, persistence, and fixture boundaries during R0.2; they are compatibility input
models, not the target production trade contract.

This change is deliberately not R0.3: `MarketSnapshot.as_of` remains unchanged here, and this brief
does not alter QuantLib global evaluation-date or fixing ownership.

## 1. Current coupling

The coupling originates in `backend/app/domain/models.py`, is collapsed by
`backend/app/market/snapshot.py:PositionMarketDataProvider`, and is then reinforced by optional
market arguments and trade-local fallbacks in `backend/app/pricing/builtin.py` and
`backend/app/pricing/quantlib.py`.

### Field classification

| Current type | Contractual economics retained in terms | Observable market state to remove from terms | Neither contractual nor an observable |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EquityPosition` | `id`, `symbol`, `quantity`, hierarchy/book metadata | `price` → `MarketSnapshot.equity_spots[symbol]` | — |
| `EquityFuturePosition` | `id`, contract symbol, `quantity`, `multiplier`, maturity, hierarchy/book | `spot`, `risk_free_rate`, `dividend_yield` → equity spot, currency curve/rate, dividend-yield market | — |
| `EuropeanOptionPosition` | `id`, underlying symbol, `quantity`, `strike`, maturity, call/put, hierarchy/book | `spot`, `volatility`, `risk_free_rate`, `dividend_yield` → spot, vol surface/scalar, rate curve, dividend-yield market | — |
| `BondPosition` | `id`, issuer/security reference, `face_value`, `quantity`, maturity, currency, hierarchy/book | `yield_rate` → discount curve/yield market | `duration` is a derived risk/pricing shortcut; it must not enter `InstrumentTerms` or authoritative market state |
| `SwapPosition` | `id`, currency, `notional`, maturity, contractual `fixed_rate`, `pay_fixed`, hierarchy/book | `market_swap_rate` → projection/discount curve market | `duration` is derived and must leave the target terms model |
| `FXForwardPosition` | `id`, pair, signed/base notional, contractual `strike`, maturity, hierarchy/book | `spot`, `domestic_rate`, `foreign_rate` → FX spot and currency curves/rates | — |
| `FXOptionPosition` | `id`, pair, base notional, `strike`, maturity, call/put, hierarchy/book | `spot`, `volatility`, `domestic_rate`, `foreign_rate` → FX spot, FX vol surface/scalar, currency curves/rates | — |
| `InterestRateFuturePosition` | `id`, currency, `quantity`, contract `pv01`/tick value, maturity, hierarchy/book | `quoted_rate`, `forward_rate` are current market/model marks and belong in the snapshot | — |
| `CapFloorPosition` | `id`, currency/index reference, notional, quantity, strike, maturity, cap/floor, payment frequency, hierarchy/book | `volatility`, `forward_rate`, `discount_rate` → IR vol quote/surface plus projection and discount curves | — |
| `SwaptionPosition` | `id`, currency/index reference, notional, quantity, strike, option expiry, swap tenor, payer/receiver, payment frequency, hierarchy/book | `volatility`, `forward_swap_rate`, `discount_rate` → IR vol quote/surface plus projection and discount curves | — |

Two current ambiguities must be made explicit while each family migrates:

- Equity instruments currently assume USD in both pricers. Add a contractual settlement/pricing
  `currency` with a compatibility default of `"USD"`; do not continue a hidden hard-coded rate key.
- `duration` on bonds and swaps cannot be relabeled as contractual. Preserve it only on legacy
  position DTOs while the family adapter retains the current approximation, then replace its use
  with a market bump/revaluation implementation under quant validation. It must be excluded from
  the target terms payload and target cache identity.

### Current authority violations

- `PositionMarketDataProvider.snapshot()` writes all bond yields, swap par rates, IR-future forwards,
  and IR-option discount marks into one `rates[currency]` entry. Iteration order decides the result.
- Both pricers use patterns such as `snapshot.get(key, position.mark)`, so a supplied but incomplete
  snapshot silently reactivates trade-local market state.
- `PricingEngine.value(..., market=None)`, `value_portfolio(..., market=None)`, and
  `shocked_value(..., market=None)` make no-market valuation part of the central interface.
- `backend/app/risk/var.py` calls `pricing.value(p)` for LINEAR/DELTA_GAMMA position P&Ls.
- `backend/app/risk/es.py` independently calls `pricing.value(p)` for approximate factor P&L.
- `backend/app/risk/historical.py` accepts `market=None` and therefore values approximate risk from
  embedded marks unless a caller happens to provide a snapshot.
- `backend/app/services/portfolio_service.py` generally invokes summary, VaR, ES, stress, factors,
  limits, and hierarchy without resolving one explicit snapshot for the calculation.
- `backend/app/pricing/cache.py:trade_cache_key()` hashes the full legacy position payload, including
  duplicated market marks, while `market_cache_key()` separately hashes snapshot content.

## 2. Smallest coherent migration by instrument family

Do not migrate all products in one commit. For each family, use the same bounded sequence:

1. Define the family `InstrumentTerms` projection (contractual fields only).
2. Define its required snapshot keys and explicit missing-market error.
3. Make Builtin and QuantLib consume those keys without trade-mark fallback.
4. Keep a legacy `*Position` → terms/sample-marks adapter at the boundary.
5. Add snapshot-authority, unchanged-trade, cache-identity, Builtin, and QuantLib parity tests.
6. Only then remove that family's embedded marks from newly authored demo/API examples.

Recommended order:

### A. Equity cash, futures, and equity options

First family because snapshot structures already exist:
`equity_spots`, `equity_vols`/`vol_surfaces`, `rates`, and `dividend_yields`.

- Terms: symbol/contract identifier, quantity, currency, multiplier where applicable, strike,
  maturity, and option side.
- Market requirements: spot; settlement-currency rate/curve and dividend yield for futures/options;
  option vol scalar/surface for options.
- Compatibility adapter reads `price`/`spot`/`volatility`/rates/dividend yield from legacy positions.
- Duplicate SPY spot or vol candidates must agree exactly (or within a documented adapter-only
  tolerance) or fail.

### B. FX forwards and options

- Terms: normalized pair, base notional, strike, maturity, option side.
- Market requirements: `fx_spots[pair]`, domestic and foreign curves/rates, and FX vol for options.
- Pair parsing must remain compatible initially, but R0.2 should centralize base/quote extraction;
  strict typed pair validation can complete under RF-011.
- Two EURUSD trades may share one EURUSD spot only if their sample marks agree.

### C. Bonds

- Terms: security/issuer reference, face, quantity, maturity, currency.
- Market requirements: currency discount curve, key-rate curve, or the existing scalar rate fallback
  in the snapshot—not `BondPosition.yield_rate`.
- A bond's embedded `yield_rate` is a sample mark only. Distinct maturity yields should seed named
  curve/key-rate nodes, not overwrite `rates["USD"]`.
- Preserve current valuation tolerances. Treat legacy `duration` as transitional adapter metadata;
  removing its Builtin DV01 use is a separately reviewed quant step inside the family migration.

### D. Swaps

- Terms: currency/index reference, notional, maturity, fixed coupon, payer/receiver.
- Market requirements: projection and discount curves; the current maturity zero resolver may remain
  temporarily, but `market_swap_rate` may not be a fallback after migration.
- A 2Y and 5Y swap quote are different tenor factors. Seed tenor nodes or fail; never collapse both
  to one scalar currency rate.
- Handle legacy `duration` with the same transitional rule as bonds.

### E. Interest-rate futures

- Terms: contract identifier, currency/index, quantity, `pv01`/tick value, maturity.
- Market requirements: quoted futures price/rate and forward/projection curve. The current
  `quoted_rate` and `forward_rate` are both market-side despite living on the position.
- If the MVP continues algebraic STIR pricing, add only the minimal snapshot quote map required;
  do not use R0.2 to replace it with a new QuantLib instrument.

### F. Caps/floors and swaptions

- Terms: currency/index, notional, quantity, strike, expiry/maturity, underlying tenor where relevant,
  option side, and payment frequency.
- Market requirements: discount/projection curves and an IR-vol quote keyed at least by product,
  currency/index, expiry, and underlying tenor. Do not use one scalar currency vol.
- Preserve the existing scoped Black-76 algorithms and schedules. Moving the three embedded market
  fields is the R0.2 change; vol-cube calibration, calendars, settlement variants, and Bermudan
  exercise remain out of scope.

## 3. Compatibility adapter design

Use explicit names and one-way adaptation:

```text
legacy Position DTO
  ├─ LegacyPositionTermsAdapter.to_trade(position)       -> Trade / InstrumentTerms
  └─ DemoSampleMarksSnapshotAdapter.snapshot(portfolio)  -> validated MarketSnapshot

production application path
  persisted/demo trade + explicitly resolved MarketSnapshot
    -> PricingEngine.value(trade, market)
```

Recommended locations:

- `backend/app/domain/trades.py`: target `Trade`/family terms contracts, introduced family by family.
- `backend/app/compat/legacy_positions.py`: legacy position-to-terms projection and legacy sample-mark
  extraction. Keeping this outside `pricing/` prevents pricers from becoming market-data adapters.
- `backend/app/market/demo_snapshot.py`: `DemoSampleMarksSnapshotAdapter` and
  `ConflictingSampleMarkError`.
- `backend/app/market/snapshot.py`: retain `PositionMarketDataProvider` temporarily as a deprecated
  alias/wrapper to the demo adapter so imports and tests can migrate gradually. It must inherit the
  new conflict behavior; it must not remain the service's production provider.

Compatibility rules:

- Continue accepting existing serialized `Position` payloads and persistence rows through R0.2.
- Do not mutate a legacy position to overlay a snapshot. Adapt terms once and pass market separately.
- `PricingEngine` production implementations require a non-optional `MarketSnapshot`.
- If direct no-market tests/demo calls must survive temporarily, expose them through a clearly named
  `LegacyDemoPricingAdapter` that first creates a validated one-position snapshot and then calls the
  production engine. Do not keep `market=None` fallback logic inside Builtin or QuantLib.
- Missing required market data raises a typed `MissingMarketDataError` naming trade ID and factor key.
  It must not silently use a legacy mark.
- Compatibility adapters are transitional and testable; add a removal condition after all sample,
  API, persistence, and test fixtures supply explicit snapshots.

## 4. Fail last-writer-wins

`DemoSampleMarksSnapshotAdapter` should collect candidates before constructing any dictionary:

```text
factor key -> [(position_id, value, legacy field), ...]
```

For each canonical factor key:

- no candidate: omit it; production pricing later raises `MissingMarketDataError` if required;
- one candidate: seed it;
- multiple equal candidates: seed once;
- multiple unequal candidates: raise `ConflictingSampleMarkError` with the factor key and all
  contributing trade IDs/values.

No dictionary assignment occurs until validation completes. Candidate equality should use exact
decimal/float equality for committed sample fixtures unless an explicit adapter tolerance is
documented; broad tolerances would hide real conflicts.

Required regression in `backend/tests/test_demo_snapshot_adapter.py`:

1. Create two USD legacy instruments, for example a bond with `yield_rate=0.041` and a swap with
   `market_swap_rate=0.043`.
2. Place them in both portfolio orders.
3. Assert both orders raise `ConflictingSampleMarkError`.
4. Assert the message/details identify `USD` and both position IDs/values.
5. Add an equal-value case that succeeds and seeds one USD rate.

The existing `RATES_MACRO_PORTFOLIO` already contains competing USD yields/swap/forward marks.
Therefore it must not simply be passed to the stricter scalar adapter. Preserve the demo by adding
an explicit demo snapshot fixture/factory that seeds appropriate USD curve/key-rate/projection
nodes, or by normalizing truly shared scalar fixture marks. The preferred route is an explicit
snapshot because different tenor quotes are legitimate market data, just not one scalar factor.

## 5. Cache identity

Target key:

```text
schema version
+ contractual trade economics fingerprint
+ MarketSnapshot.content_hash()
+ pricing configuration
+ valuation/as-of conventions when R0.3 makes them authoritative
```

Changes belong in `backend/app/pricing/cache.py`:

- Replace full legacy `position.model_dump()` hashing with a family terms/economics projection.
- Exclude `price`, `spot`, vol, current yields/forwards/par rates, and derived `duration`.
- Keep economically relevant identity and economics: trade ID if cache entries are trade-scoped,
  quantity/notional, currency/pair, strike, maturity, side, multiplier, fixed coupon, payment
  frequency, and other true contract terms.
- Continue using snapshot content rather than labels (`id`/`as_of`) during R0.2. R0.3 must revisit
  typed `as_of` because date conventions can change valuation even with equal marks.
- Bump the cache-key schema prefix from `v1` when projection semantics change.
- Do not cache a production valuation with `market=None`.
- A compatibility no-market call must first normalize to `(terms, validated demo snapshot)` or bypass
  the production cache. Omitting legacy marks from the trade key while retaining `market:none`
  would create incorrect collisions.

Tests in `backend/tests/test_pricing_cache.py` must prove:

- changing only a legacy embedded mark does not change the terms fingerprint;
- changing quantity/strike/maturity/fixed coupon does change it;
- changing snapshot content changes the valuation key;
- equal economics plus equal snapshot content gives the same key despite different legacy marks;
- no production cache key accepts `None` market.

## 6. What not to do

- Do not rewrite `domain/models.py` wholesale or change every API/persistence DTO in one release.
- Do not build an instrument plugin framework, service locator, or dynamic registry in R0.2.
- Do not combine RF-012's eventual small capability registry with this first migration slice.
- Do not introduce microservices, vendor market feeds, a second pricing library, or C++ business logic.
- Do not redesign curve bootstrap, scenario models, vol calibration, or historical factor panels.
- Do not change QuantLib session locking, evaluation dates, or fixing ownership; that is R0.3.
- Do not silently map distinct tenor/basis/index quotes to one scalar rate merely to preserve demos.
- Do not copy a snapshot's marks back into `Position.model_copy()` as the target design. The current
  QuantLib overlay is temporary implementation debt, not the desired boundary.
- Do not weaken existing golden tolerances or update expected values without quant review.

## 7. Recommended first implementation slice

The first slice should establish market authority and stop silent data corruption before introducing
all new family terms classes. It is intentionally executable without R0.3.

### Slice R0.2-A — explicit snapshot path and validated demo bridge

Files:

- Add `backend/app/market/demo_snapshot.py` with `DemoSampleMarksSnapshotAdapter`,
  candidate collection, `ConflictingSampleMarkError`, and no last-writer-wins writes.
- Update `backend/app/market/snapshot.py` so `PositionMarketDataProvider` is a temporary deprecated
  compatibility wrapper around the validated demo adapter.
- Update `backend/app/interfaces/pricing.py` to define/document the production non-optional market
  contract and isolate no-market behavior in a named compatibility adapter.
- Update `backend/app/risk/var.py`, `backend/app/risk/historical.py`, and `backend/app/risk/es.py` so
  approximate and full-revaluation paths value every trade against the same explicit base snapshot.
- Update `backend/app/services/portfolio_service.py` to resolve one snapshot at the application
  boundary and pass it through summary/VaR/ES first. Do not yet fan this into unrelated R0.6/R0.7
  execution refactors.
- Update `backend/app/pricing/cache.py` to require snapshot identity and add a versioned
  trade-economics projection for the first migrated equity family; retain explicit compatibility
  handling for unmigrated legacy families.
- Add an explicit snapshot factory for demo portfolios in `backend/app/sample.py` (or a narrowly
  named adjacent module) so mixed USD demo books use deliberate curves/projection marks rather than
  the conflict-prone scalar adapter.

Tests:

- Add `backend/tests/test_demo_snapshot_adapter.py`: unequal shared-factor conflict in both orders;
  equal duplicate acceptance; mixed USD demo explicit-snapshot success.
- Add `backend/tests/test_market_authority.py`: snapshot A/B changes valuation while the legacy trade
  object remains byte/model-dump identical; missing required marks fail; trade-local marks cannot
  override supplied snapshot.
- Extend `backend/tests/test_pricing_cache.py` with economics-vs-market identity tests.
- Extend `backend/tests/test_var_es_golden.py`,
  `backend/tests/test_approx_pnl_units_golden.py`, and
  `backend/tests/test_full_reval_golden.py` to assert the explicit snapshot reaches all methodologies.
- Preserve `backend/tests/test_curve_pricing.py`, `backend/tests/test_quantlib_golden.py`,
  `backend/tests/test_quantlib_pricing.py`, `backend/tests/test_ir_options_pricing.py`, and
  `backend/tests/test_demo_portfolios.py`; update fixture construction, not financial expectations.

Why this is first: it removes silent overwrite and no-market production risk calls, creates a safe
compatibility bridge, and fixes cache ownership without requiring typed `as_of`, process-global
QuantLib changes, a vol-cube model, or an all-instrument domain rewrite. Family terms can then migrate
in the order in section 2 behind an already-correct application boundary.

## 8. Compatibility concerns and verification list

### Compatibility concerns

- Existing API bodies, persisted portfolio JSON, OpenAPI examples, demo artifacts, and tests instantiate
  required legacy mark fields. Keep their DTOs readable throughout R0.2.
- Making the demo adapter reject conflicts will expose the current rates demo immediately; land its
  explicit snapshot in the same change as conflict validation.
- Direct `BuiltinPricingEngine().value(position)` and QuantLib equivalent are common in quant unit
  tests. Migrate them to explicit snapshots or the named legacy adapter in controlled batches.
- The current QuantLib adapter overlays market values into a copied position. This does not mutate the
  caller, but it preserves the wrong conceptual direction. It may remain internally for one family
  slice only if tests prove snapshot authority; remove it family by family.
- `MarketSnapshot.content_hash()` excludes `id` and `as_of`. Preserve this behavior in R0.2 and flag
  the date-dependent cache correction for R0.3.
- Existing fallback curves/scalars are relied upon by pricing parity tests. “Fallback” may remain from
  richer snapshot structures to simpler snapshot structures; fallback from snapshot to trade marks
  may not.
- Demo artifacts may change if the current last writer was accidentally determining rates. Do not
  bless changed numbers automatically; compare against explicit-snapshot expectations and obtain
  quant validation.

### Required tests before each family handoff

- Legacy payload parses and projects to the expected contractual terms.
- Terms dump contains no listed observable market fields.
- Snapshot A and B produce expected valuation changes with the same immutable trade.
- Missing required factor raises a typed error, not a fallback valuation.
- Duplicate demo marks: equal accepted; unequal rejected independent of position order.
- Builtin and QuantLib paths consume the same explicit snapshot under existing parity tolerances.
- LINEAR, DELTA_GAMMA, and FULL_REVALUATION all use the supplied base snapshot.
- ES position and factor contributions use the supplied base snapshot.
- Cache hit/miss behavior separates terms changes from snapshot changes.
- Market bumps do not alter trade terms or their fingerprint.
- Existing curve, surface, sensitivity, stress, attribution, hierarchy, persistence, API, and demo
  regressions remain green for the affected family.
- QuantLib hard-gate job executes with zero skips. No R0.2 acceptance claim depends on R0.3 concurrency
  tests passing.

## Acceptance criteria for R0.2

- Production risk application paths always supply a `MarketSnapshot`.
- A supplied snapshot is authoritative; missing factors fail and legacy marks cannot override it.
- Shared sample factors cannot be assigned competing values silently.
- Legacy `Position` payloads remain accepted through explicit compatibility adapters.
- Cache identity is contractual economics plus snapshot content plus pricing configuration.
- Existing numerical expectations are preserved unless a separately reviewed defect is identified.
- No R0.3 QuantLib session work, plugin framework, or broad domain/API rewrite is included.
