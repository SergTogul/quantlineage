## Verdict: APPROVE

Every current `Position` family handled by `trade_cache_key` has an
explicit `*Terms` model and an allow-list `isinstance` branch.
`terms_from_position` copies identifier, type, quantity/notional,
currency or pair, strike, maturity, pay/receive, multiplier,
contractual `fixed_rate`, `pv01`, payment frequency, option side, and
hierarchy placement. Live observables
(`price` / `spot` / `vol` / `yield_rate` / `market_swap_rate` /
`quoted_rate` / `forward_rate` / `discount_rate` / `domestic_rate` /
`foreign_rate` / `risk_free_rate` / `dividend_yield` /
`forward_swap_rate` / derived `duration`) are not fields on any terms
class. Changing any of those marks on a Position leaves terms equal;
changing strike, maturity, notional, or quantity does not. Unknown
types raise `TypeError` instead of dumping the Position. `*Position`
DTO mark fields were not removed or made optional. `cache.py` and
pricing `value()` have no diff and do not import this module. FINDINGS
RF-001 remains **IN PROGRESS**.

This slice is enough to treat **typed InstrumentTerms extraction as
present**. It is **not** enough to close RF-001: compatibility
`*Position` DTOs still embed marks, and production cache / `value()`
still consume those DTOs.

## Findings

### 1. VALID — Minor — Incomplete `mark_update` on two option rows

- File: `backend/tests/test_instrument_terms.py`
  (`_FAMILY_CASES` european_option / fx_option)
- What's wrong: Identity-test `mark_update` for equity options is
  `{spot, volatility}` only; for FX options `{spot, volatility}` only.
  `risk_free_rate` / `dividend_yield` and `domestic_rate` /
  `foreign_rate` are listed in `mark_fields` (name-absence check) and
  are excluded in production (probed) but a future copy of those
  fields onto terms would not fail the identity parametrize row.
- Why it matters: Coverage hole, not a leftover mark. Bond / swap /
  FX-forward / IR rows already flip every excluded mark.
- Suggested fix: Add the omitted rates/yields to those two
  `mark_update` dicts. Do not treat this as open terms-model work.
