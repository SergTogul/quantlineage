## Findings

No Critical or Important leftover on the factory as-of cache claim.

Residual (not blockers; not deferred-Minor reopen):

- The as-of parser is copied in `cache.py` and `quantlib.py`. They match today.
  A later one-sided edit could recreate Attack 3. R0.3.1 is the right place to
  collapse them.
- `isinstance(as_of, date)` is true for `datetime`, so a constructed datetime
  object keys as `as_of:2018-01-01T00:00:00` while `date` / `YYYY-MM-DD` keys
  as `as_of:2018-01-01`. That is an extra miss, not a wrong-PV hit.
  `MarketSnapshot.as_of` remains `str` on the application path.
