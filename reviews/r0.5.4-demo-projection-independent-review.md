## Findings

### 1. VALID — Minor — `is_per_name_per_tenor_panel` is “not four_macro_demo”, not an explicit False

- Files: `backend/app/risk/historical_data.py` L107–109
- What's wrong: The mixin returns `self.projection != HistoricalDatasetProjection.FOUR_MACRO_DEMO`. Today there is only one enum member, and every shipped loader passes that member, so live objects are `False` (probe). A later enum value, or a raw non-enum string stored on the dataclass field, would report `True` without a per-name/per-tenor panel existing. `is_per_name_per_tenor_panel` / `aggregate_factors` are also mixin-only; the protocol requires `projection` only.
- Why it matters: The leftover under review is “do not present four columns as a factor panel.” That holds for the shipped objects. This is a residual default, not a live hole.
- Suggested fix: `return False` while `FOUR_MACRO_DEMO` is the only projection, or put an explicit `False` on the protocol/default. Optional. Not required to keep this leftover.
