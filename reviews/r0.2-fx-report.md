## Summary
When a `MarketSnapshot` is supplied, `FXForwardPosition` and `FXOptionPosition`
now require snapshot FX marks in both `BuiltinPricingEngine` and the QuantLib
overlay. Missing keys raise `MissingMarketDataError` with
`factor_key="fx_spots[<pair>]"`, `"fx_vols[<pair>]"` (options),
`"rates[<domestic>]"` (`pair[-3:]`), or `"rates[<foreign>]"` (`pair[:3]`).
Option vol prefers a usable `vol_surfaces[pair]` quote, then requires
`fx_vols[pair]`. Trade-local `spot` / `volatility` / `domestic_rate` /
`foreign_rate` remain only when `market is None`. Equity fail-closed behavior
and bond/swap/IR fallbacks were not changed. Canned `global-macro` demo
aggregates already seed EURUSD spot, vol, USD, and EUR; a lock test now
guards those keys.

## Public/interface changes
- No DTO, API, or snapshot schema change.
- Production pricing contract for FX forwards: a supplied snapshot must contain
  `fx_spots[pair]`, `rates[pair[-3:]]` (domestic), and `rates[pair[:3]]`
  (foreign), or valuation raises `MissingMarketDataError`.
- Production pricing contract for FX options: the same plus a usable
  `vol_surfaces[pair]` quote or `fx_vols[pair]`.
- `required_fx_option_vol` is a new shared helper next to
  `required_equity_option_vol`. It does not accept a trade-mark fallback.
- `MarketSnapshot.rates` still defaults to `{"USD": 0.04}`. A snapshot that
  omits the `rates=` argument is therefore not a missing-USD-rate case; tests
  that isolate a missing domestic rate pass `rates={}` or `rates={"EUR": …}`.

## Numerical conventions
- Units: FX forward PV remains `N * (F - K) * DF_d` with
  `F = S * exp((rd - rf) * T)` (continuous CIP). FX option PV remains
  Garman–Kohlhagen unit price times `notional_base`; vega remains per 1 vol
  point; FX delta remains cash delta (`N * unit_delta * S`).
- Sign convention: unchanged (long forward / long call positive in rising
  spot; higher rd raises long-forward PV; higher rf lowers it).
- Day count/calendar if relevant: unchanged; this slice is market-authority
  only. QuantLib still uses FlatForward continuous Actual365Fixed and
  `_maturity_date` calendar rounding.
- Tolerances/reference: CIP identity `rel=1e-12, abs=1e-9` vs Builtin and
  algebraic `N*(F-K)*exp(-rd*T)`. Option Builtin-vs-snapshot-local
  `rel=1e-12`. QuantLib vs Builtin option `rel=5e-3` (existing date-rounding
  band). Authority tests also assert inequality versus trade-local marks and
  unchanged `model_dump`. No golden PV rewritten.

## Known limitations / risks
- Bond, swap, and IR families still use `.get` / trade-mark fallbacks.
- `market is None` still prices from embedded marks (compatibility path;
  omitted-`market=` inference was out of scope).
- Pair parsing remains `pair[-3:]` / `pair[:3]`; typed pair validation is
  RF-011.
- `MarketSnapshot.rates` still defaults to `{"USD": 0.04}`. Omitting `rates=`
  is not the same as a missing USD domestic rate.
- FX cache identity remains `legacy_position_v1` (embedded marks still hashed).
  Terms fingerprint for this family was not migrated in this slice.
- Suites outside the required list that price FX against a supplied snapshot
  missing EUR/USD rates or EURUSD vol will now raise.
