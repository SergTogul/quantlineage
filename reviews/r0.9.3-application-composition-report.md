## Summary
`PortfolioService` is no longer assigned at `app.api.deps` import time.
Lifespan calls `build_portfolio_service()`, stores the instance on
`app.state.portfolio_service`, and constructs `RiskRunWorker` with that same
object. `get_portfolio_service(request)` reads `app.state` like
`get_risk_run_worker` and **fails closed (503)** when `app.state.portfolio_service`
is missing. `app.main.service` is a thin `__getattr__` alias to `app.state` only
(no module-global factory fallback). No DI container. Risk numbers unchanged.

**Disposition: IN PROGRESS** — do not CLOSE RF-010. Dual-use
`AttributionRequest` / `RiskChangeAttributionRequest` / `WhatIfRequest` remain
in domain. Independent review of the fail-closed follow-up is pending.

TDD: module-global `portfolio_service = build_portfolio_service()` failed first;
`get_portfolio_service` lacked `request`; `app.state.portfolio_service` was
